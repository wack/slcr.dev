## 2026-10-29: SLCR launches

Today we're launching SLCR (Sliceable, Linked, Checkable Requirements, 
pronounced *slicer*), an open format for writing software requirements for 
both people and coding agents. We're the team at Wack, and we built SLCR 
because our own agents kept drifting from the spec over time. If you work on 
a long-lived codebase and you're handing more of the implementation to agents, 
SLCR gives you a way to check that what comes back still matches what you 
asked for.

You can try it today. The
[getting started tutorial](/docs/getting-started/tutorial) takes you from
installing the CLI to your first spec, and the
[agent skill](/docs/getting-started/installation#install-the-agent-skill)
teaches your agents to read and write SLCR.

Everything is open source, and the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/wack/slcr)
is where the work happens. If something breaks or doesn't fit how your team 
works, open an issue there and tell us about it. Pull requests are welcome too!
