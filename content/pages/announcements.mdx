## 2026-10-27: SLCR launches

Today we're launching SLCR (Sliceable, Linked, Checkable Requirements), an
open format for writing software requirements that people and coding agents
can both work from. We're the team at Wack and we built SLCR because our own
agents kept drifting from long specs. If you work on a long-lived codebase and
you're handing more of the implementation to agents, SLCR gives you a way to
check that what comes back still matches what you asked for.

You can try it today. The
[getting started tutorial](/docs/getting-started/tutorial) takes you from
installing the CLI to your first spec, and if you use Claude, there's a
[skill](/docs/getting-started/installation#install-the-agent-skill) that
teaches your agents to read and write SLCR.

Everything is open source, and the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/wack/slcr)
is where the work happens. If something breaks or doesn't fit how your team
works, open an issue there and tell us about it. Pull requests welcome!
